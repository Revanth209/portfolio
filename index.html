Featured project

Ravan, a TallyPrime automation app

A Windows desktop application I designed and developed with Electron and React. It connects to TallyPrime on the same computer and automates the most repetitive parts of an accountant's day.

  • Ravan AI: an offline assistant for GST, income-tax, TDS and Tally queries, with document attachment.
  • Upload Bills: imports sales and purchase bills from Excel, PDF or photos and posts them to Tally, creating missing ledgers and stock items.
  • Bank Statement: imports statements from Excel, PDF or scans and posts the entries to Tally.
  • Tally Reports: extracts sales, purchase and stock summary data with selectable fields and exports it to Excel.
